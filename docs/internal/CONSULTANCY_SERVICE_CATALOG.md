# Khepra Consultancy Service Catalog
## "Weapons Systems" for Enterprise Risk & Compliance

This catalog defines the tiered service offerings of the Khepra Consultancy. It anchors our proprietary "Weapons Systems" technical capabilities to market-recognized categories (CMMC, NIST, SOC 2), ensuring buyers understand the value proposition while retaining our premium, high-tech differentiation.

---

### I. Strategic Tier: Business Planning & Analysis
**A.K.A. Strategic Weapons Systems**
*Market Alignment: Readiness & Regulatory Alignment*

#### 1. Mission Assurance Modeling (MAM)
**Market-Facing:** CMMC / NIST Readiness & Mission Alignment Assessment

*   **What it is:** A fixed-scope engagement that ingests your mission, growth plans, and financial constraints to show where security and compliance either support or block the business.
*   **Inputs:** Mission/vision decks, budget and headcount plans, major program roadmaps.
*   **Tech:** Agent-based parsing of natural language strategy into "Goal Vectors."
*   **Outputs:**
    *   **Executive Strategic Alignment Scorecard:** "Where your 12–24 month strategy is at risk due to cyber/compliance gaps."
    *   **Prioritized Roadmap:** 3–5 high-impact initiatives tied to CMMC/NIST/STIG outcomes (e.g., "Achieve CMMC L2 readiness on Program X in 9 months").

#### 2. Regulatory Deconfliction Engine
**Market-Facing:** Regulatory & Contractual Requirements Harmonization

*   **What it is:** A focused analysis of all the laws, frameworks, and contract clauses that touch your programs (CMMC, NIST 800-171, DFARS, GDPR, export controls) to eliminate contradictory guidance.
*   **Inputs:** Contracts, SOWs, data-processing agreements, policy set, current control catalog ("Laws, Regulations, Contracts").
*   **Tech:** Khepra "Lawyer" Agents mapping obligations to technical constraints.
*   **Outputs:**
    *   **Unified Compliance Directive:** A single, non-conflicting set of requirements for Engineering, IT, and Security.
    *   **Prioritization Tags:** Clear "must do / nice to have / not applicable" tags per program to optimize spend.

### II. Operational Tier: Process Analysis & Operations
**A.K.A. Operational Weapons Systems**
*Market Alignment: Architecture & Supply Chain Assessment*

#### 3. CONOPS Digital Twin
**Market-Facing:** Security Architecture & Operating Model Assessment

*   **What it is:** A modeled "digital twin" of how your organization actually works—who touches what data, where it flows, and which teams own which risks.
*   **Inputs:** Org charts, "Stakeholder Roles (RASCI)", process diagrams, CMDB/asset list, ticketing/workflow exports.
*   **Tech:** Graph-based modeling of organization structure and flows.
*   **Outputs:**
    *   **Friction & Exposure Heatmap:** Identifying where "Responsible" parties lack the "Resources" to secure the flow (e.g., "Ops is accountable for enclave X but has no change control on vendor Y").
    *   **Operating Model Recommendations:** Specific changes to make CMMC/NIST controls enforceable instead of theoretical.

#### 4. Supply Chain Hunter (SCRM)
**Market-Facing:** Third- & Fourth-Party Cyber Risk Assessment

*   **What it is:** A deep-scan forensic audit of your "Outsourced Business Processes" and vendor ecosystem. We find the "Toxic Trust."
*   **Inputs:** Vendor list, contracts and SLAs, SBOMs, major integrations (APIs, data feeds, identity).
*   **Tech:** Recursive dependency scanners (`pkg/arsenal`) targeting 3rd and 4th party risk.
*   **Outputs:**
    *   **Vendor Exposure Graph:** Ranks suppliers by technical blast radius and contractual risk.
    *   **SCRM Remediation Playbook:** Which vendors to harden, monitor differently, or replace.

### III. Tactical Tier: Process Implementation & Technology
**A.K.A. Tactical Weapons Systems**
*Market Alignment: Zero-Trust & Code Assurance*

#### 5. Zero-Trust Enforcement (ZTE)
**Market-Facing:** Zero-Trust & Configuration Drift Assessment

*   **What it is:** A targeted review of your actual configs (cloud, endpoint, network) against your stated policies and SSP, wired for ongoing enforcement where possible.
*   **Inputs:** Cloud configuration exports (AWS/Azure), identity policies, SSP, logging/SIEM data.
*   **Tech:** `pkg/connectors` running agents to detect drift in real-time.
*   **Outputs:**
    *   **Drift Validation Report:** Shows exactly where reality diverges from policy and which configs impact CMMC/NIST posture.
    *   **Hardening Plan:** Prioritized fixes (short-term) and architectural changes (long-term). Mention of Khepra automated enforcement capabilities.

#### 6. Code Lineage & PQC Attestation
**Market-Facing:** Secure SDLC, IP Lineage & Quantum-Safe Crypto Review

*   **What it is:** A combined appsec and IP-assurance engagement that traces code provenance, evaluates libraries and cryptography, and provides a clean bill of health for investors/regulators.
*   **Inputs:** Source repos, CI/CD configs, dependency manifests/SBOMs.
*   **Tech:** Algorithmic proof of authorship (AR 27-60) and PQC analysis.
*   **Outputs:**
    *   **IP Purity & Lineage Summary:** Where code is clean vs. obligated (OSS/Third-party), identifying valuation risks.
    *   **Quantum-Safe Roadmap:** Plan to migrate sensitive data flows to PQ-ready primitives.

### IV. The "Weapons System" Deliverable: Khepra Causal Risk Attestation
**Market-Facing:** Board-Ready Causal Risk Attestation (The "Godfather" Report)

*   **What it is:** A single narrative that connects strategy, architecture, vendors, and code into "material risk" language for boards and regulators.
*   **Structure:**
    1.  **Strategic Context:** What the business is trying to achieve.
    2.  **Operational Reality:** How the organization actually operates.
    3.  **Tactical Proof:** Concrete evidence (configs, logs) of divergence.
    4.  **Interventions:** The 5-10 moves to ensure defensible readiness.
