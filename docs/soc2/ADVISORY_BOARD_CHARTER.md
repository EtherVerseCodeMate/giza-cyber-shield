## 4. Advisory Responsibilities (CC1.2)

### 4.1 Quarterly Security Briefings

- **Frequency**: Quarterly (Q1: March 31, Q2: June 30, Q3: September 30, Q4: December 31)
- **Format**: Founder sends a 1-page security posture summary using the template at `docs/soc2/QUARTERLY_SECURITY_BRIEFING_TEMPLATE.md`
- **Advisor Action**: Acknowledge receipt in writing (email to creatinghappyhumans@gmail.com is sufficient)
- **Evidence**: Email threads retained in `docs/soc2/evidence/CC1.2/`

### 4.2 Annual Risk Assessment Review

- **Frequency**: Annual, before the risk assessment is finalised
- **Document**: `docs/soc2/RISK_ASSESSMENT_2026.md` (and future-year equivalents)
- **Advisor Action**: Review, provide written feedback, and sign/countersign the document
- **Evidence**: Signed risk assessment + any review correspondence retained in `docs/soc2/evidence/CC1.2/`

### 4.3 HIGH/CRITICAL Risk Acceptance

- **Trigger**: Any risk scored HIGH or CRITICAL that the Founder proposes to accept rather than remediate
- **Advisor Action**: Provide written concurrence (email sufficient) before risk is formally accepted
- **Evidence**: Written concurrence email retained in `docs/soc2/evidence/CC1.2/`

### 4.4 Policy Review and Countersignature

- **Scope**: Major security policies: ISP-001 (Information Security Policy), IRP-001 (Incident Response Plan), BCP-001 (Business Continuity Plan)
- **Advisor Action**: Review and countersign (email approval sufficient for electronic records) before policies are finalised
- **Evidence**: Signed policy documents or email approvals retained with policy documents

### 4.5 P1 Incident Notification

- **Trigger**: Any P1 (Critical) incident affecting customer data or system availability
- **Timeline**: Advisor notified within 24 hours of incident declaration
- **Method**: Email or phone
- **Evidence**: Incident report in `docs/soc2/evidence/CC7.4/` noting notification timestamp

### 4.6 Strategic Governance Consultation

The Advisor may be consulted on:
- Significant technology architecture changes
- New vendor relationships (Tier 1 — data processors)
- Regulatory compliance questions
- Major business decisions with security or privacy implications

## 5. Meeting Cadence

| Meeting Type | Frequency | Format | Duration | Quorum |
|-------------|-----------|--------|----------|--------|
| Quarterly Security Briefing | Quarterly | Async (written) or video call | 30 min or async | Founder + Advisor |
| Annual Governance Review | Annual | Video call | 60 min | Both |
| Ad-hoc (P1, HIGH risk) | As needed | Phone / email | As needed | Both |

**Annual Governance Review agenda** (minimum):
1. Review prior year's security posture and incident summary
2. Review and approve updated risk assessment
3. Review and countersign updated policies
4. Discuss upcoming audit readiness
5. Agree on next year's oversight schedule

---

## 6. Information Rights

The Advisor shall receive:

1. Quarterly security briefings (within 5 days of quarter-end)
2. Annual risk assessment (before finalisation)
3. Notification of P1 incidents within 24 hours
4. Notification of HIGH/CRITICAL risk acceptance proposals before execution
5. Notification of major product releases (as provided in the authority matrix)
6. Copy of any external audit report or significant security finding

---

## 7. Advisor Limitations and Independence

The Advisor:
- Has no operational authority or execution responsibility for security controls
- Provides oversight and governance review only
- Does not approve or reject individual technical decisions (only major policies and risk acceptance)
- Is not a fiduciary director of the Company
- Retains independence and may resign if independence is impaired
